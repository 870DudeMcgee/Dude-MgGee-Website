## Decision

**Accept the reproduced citation counts as facts about the released dataset. Retain the experiments as limited, operator-reported findings. Do not accept the video's universal retrieval mechanism, profitability, research-publisher identity, or recommendation-impact claims as established.**

The evidence supports investigating both legitimate brand visibility and research reliability. It does not establish a useful marketing return for our brands, nor prove that mass-generated pages changed recommendations. Reproducing a dataset's arithmetic does not authenticate its collection.

Classification: **REPRODUCED** means independently calculated from downloaded records; **SUPPORTED** means a primary artifact supports the narrow statement; **REPORTED** means published by the investigator without independent raw-data reproduction here; **UNKNOWN** means not established in this bounded investigation.

## Observable outcomes and attribution limits — ticket #4

**Decision: measure only observable stages; label unobserved stages unknown.** These are proposed operational definitions for later evaluation, not measured results for an owner-selected brand. Crawling, citation, mention, recommendation and referral are separate observations, not a guaranteed funnel. A recommendation can occur without a citation or a fresh fetch; a citation can support a competitor. No proprietary engine logs or hidden retrieval/ranking states are assumed.

| Outcome | Observable success and unit | Observed failure versus unknown | Attribution limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Crawling / fetching | An owned server/CDN access record of a request to a particular URL, with timestamp, status and requester evidence. Distinguish automatic crawling from user-triggered fetching; report requests and unique URLs separately. Validate a claimed bot against the provider's published identification evidence where available. | A failed request is a fetch failure for that URL/time. No recorded request means no observed fetch within retained log coverage; absent/incomplete logs make fetching **UNKNOWN**. | A user-agent string alone can be spoofed. A successful request does not establish indexing, use in an answer, a person viewing it or a referral. [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) distinguishes search crawling, training crawling and ChatGPT-User visits for certain user actions. |
| Citation | A saved answer visibly links or attributes a source URL. Preserve the answer, engine/surface, prompt, timestamp and cited URL; count citation occurrences separately from answers containing at least one target citation and from unique URLs/domains. | No target source in a complete captured answer is a non-citation for that run. An incomplete capture or unavailable source list is **UNKNOWN**. | A citation proves displayed attribution, not factual support, endorsement, independence, reading depth or a click. The released fixture reproduces occurrences but lacks full answer text; it cannot establish citation-to-claim support or unseen retrieval. |
| Brand mention | A saved answer names the resolved brand/product in answer text, using declared aliases and manual disambiguation. Count answers with a mention; classify favorable, neutral and negative context separately. A domain in a source list alone does not count as an answer-text mention. | No name/alias in complete answer text is no mention for that run. Ambiguous entities or missing answer text are **UNKNOWN**. | Naming a brand in a comparison, warning or quotation is not recommending it. [Ahrefs' experiment](https://ahrefs.com/blog/self-promotional-content-ai-seo-experiment/) separately measures mentions and citations; its operator-reported results are not independent causal proof. |
| Recommendation | A saved answer affirmatively proposes the resolved brand/product as a choice for the user's stated need. Record the supporting answer span, suitability, caveats and any rank; count answers with a positive recommendation separately from mentions. | An explicit rejection or a complete answer with no affirmative selection is no positive recommendation for that run. Ambiguous wording or missing answer text is **UNKNOWN**. | A list titled “recommended options” can qualify; a bare list of entities cannot without affirmative context. This is a human coding rule, not access to the model's intent. A recommendation can be inaccurate or unsuitable, so accuracy needs separate primary-source checking in the later evaluation. |
| Attributable referral | A human landing visit with retained evidence of origin, such as a recorded AI-service referrer or a controlled observed click linked to that visit. Count visits/sessions under a stated deduplication rule; distinguish general service origin from linkage to one saved answer. Bot fetches do not qualify. | A controlled observed session with no outbound visit has no referral in that observation window. No attributed analytics visit in uncontrolled traffic means no **observed attributable** referral, not proof that nobody visited. Missing origin or answer linkage is **UNKNOWN**. | A service referrer does not identify the prompt, recommendation, or exact answer. Tags only carry the identity assigned to them; neither a tag nor referrer proves causal lift. [Google Analytics traffic-source documentation](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/11242841) describes referrer/campaign collection and direct traffic when referral information is unavailable. Referrals are visits, not purchases or profit. |

### Two concrete counterexamples

- **Citation without recommendation:** Ahrefs reports answers that linked its conference-promoting pages yet omitted Ahrefs Evolve and recommended other conferences. This is an operator-reported observation, already qualified in the matrix above. An illustrative answer with that structure would say “Consider Conference B” and cite an Evolve-owned comparison page: the source gets a citation while Conference B gets the recommendation. The wording is illustrative, not a recovered raw response.
- **Recommendation without attributable referral:** In an illustrative controlled session, the saved answer says “Conference B fits your SEO learning goal,” but the participant closes the answer without clicking during the observation window. Recommendation = yes; observed referral = no. Alternatively, a later manually entered website visit with no retained origin evidence leaves AI attribution unknown. These are counterexamples permitted by the definitions, not reported experiment outcomes. Pew's [observed Google browsing study](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) separately measures source clicks; it does not measure this hypothetical recommendation or generalize to other engines.

### What counts can and cannot answer

For answer-based rates, declare the target entity/URL set, prompt set, engine/surface, observation dates and number of complete eligible answers. Report positive counts, observed negatives, unknown captures and exclusions separately. Repeated prompts, multiple citations in one answer, model tiers and shared-owner domains must not become independent people or corroboration. Request counts, answer counts and visit counts use different denominators; do not calculate stage-to-stage conversion without linked observations and compatible coverage. This is a measurement contract for later planning, not a selection of engines, prompts or sample size.

The reproduced 181/7,534 describes citation share in the released sample. It does not reveal brand-mention rate, recommendation lift, audience reach, attributable referrals, conversions, revenue, profit or research accuracy. Even authentic before/after visibility increases need a credible comparison to support causal claims. For the marketing track, useful value requires an owner-selected objective and evidence of appropriate recommendations and/or attributable qualified visits, with conversions and costs measured separately if business return is claimed. For the reliability track, value requires independently checked claim correctness and source support; popularity or citation volume cannot substitute for those checks.

Primary-source basis checked September 30, 2026: provider bot documentation, Ahrefs' own experiment, Google Analytics' collection documentation and Pew's own study. The coding rules and hypothetical examples above are our proposed definitions/inferences. **UNKNOWN:** unobserved engine internals, fixture collection authenticity and business/accuracy impact. **OWNER DECISION:** brands, research system, engine coverage, evaluation budget and success threshold. Ticket #4 resolves the distinctions only; tickets #5–#7 own subsequent choices and safeguards.

## Assertions excluded from the resolution

- A crawler hit proves only a request, not an answered user question, citation, recommendation or conversion. The video's 320-question/one-human anecdote lacks an authenticated response-to-log linkage here.
- Specific universal rules that engines rank author bios/freshness, count sources instead of authenticating them, or evade Google filters because visitors are absent were not demonstrated by these studies. They are mechanistic hypotheses.
- The Zapier 50,000-page and two-thirds self-ranking assertions were not central numerical artifacts reproduced here; they cannot justify implementation without their own methods/denominators.
- Shared-source deduplication, primary-source preference and retrieval logging remain candidate safeguards for ticket #6. None of these studies measures their effectiveness; operator agreement alone does not prove a claim true.
