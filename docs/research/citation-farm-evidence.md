## Decision

**Accept the reproduced citation counts as facts about the released dataset. Retain the experiments as limited, operator-reported findings. Do not accept the video's universal retrieval mechanism, profitability, research-publisher identity, or recommendation-impact claims as established.**

The evidence supports investigating both legitimate brand visibility and research reliability. It does not establish a useful marketing return for our brands, nor prove that mass-generated pages changed recommendations. Reproducing a dataset's arithmetic does not authenticate its collection.

Classification: **REPRODUCED** means independently calculated from downloaded records; **SUPPORTED** means a primary artifact supports the narrow statement; **REPORTED** means published by the investigator without independent raw-data reproduction here; **UNKNOWN** means not established in this bounded investigation.

**Decision: measure only observable stages; label unobserved stages unknown.** These are proposed operational definitions for later evaluation, not measured results for an owner-selected brand. Crawling, citation, mention, recommendation and referral are separate observations, not a guaranteed funnel. A recommendation can occur without a citation or a fresh fetch; a citation can support a competitor. No proprietary engine logs or hidden retrieval/ranking states are assumed.

### Two concrete counterexamples

- **Citation without recommendation:** Ahrefs reports answers that linked its conference-promoting pages yet omitted Ahrefs Evolve and recommended other conferences. This is an operator-reported observation, already qualified in the matrix above. An illustrative answer with that structure would say “Consider Conference B” and cite an Evolve-owned comparison page: the source gets a citation while Conference B gets the recommendation. The wording is illustrative, not a recovered raw response.
- **Recommendation without attributable referral:** In an illustrative controlled session, the saved answer says “Conference B fits your SEO learning goal,” but the participant closes the answer without clicking during the observation window. Recommendation = yes; observed referral = no. Alternatively, a later manually entered website visit with no retained origin evidence leaves AI attribution unknown. These are counterexamples permitted by the definitions, not reported experiment outcomes. Pew's [observed Google browsing study](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) separately measures source clicks; it does not measure this hypothetical recommendation or generalize to other engines.

### What counts can and cannot answer

For answer-based rates, declare the target entity/URL set, prompt set, engine/surface, observation dates and number of complete eligible answers. Report positive counts, observed negatives, unknown captures and exclusions separately. Repeated prompts, multiple citations in one answer, model tiers and shared-owner domains must not become independent people or corroboration. Request counts, answer counts and visit counts use different denominators; do not calculate stage-to-stage conversion without linked observations and compatible coverage. This is a measurement contract for later planning, not a selection of engines, prompts or sample size.

The reproduced 181/7,534 describes citation share in the released sample. It does not reveal brand-mention rate, recommendation lift, audience reach, attributable referrals, conversions, revenue, profit or research accuracy. Even authentic before/after visibility increases need a credible comparison to support causal claims. For the marketing track, useful value requires an owner-selected objective and evidence of appropriate recommendations and/or attributable qualified visits, with conversions and costs measured separately if business return is claimed. For the reliability track, value requires independently checked claim correctness and source support; popularity or citation volume cannot substitute for those checks.

Primary-source basis checked September 30, 2026: provider bot documentation, Ahrefs' own experiment, Google Analytics' collection documentation and Pew's own study. The coding rules and hypothetical examples above are our proposed definitions/inferences. **UNKNOWN:** unobserved engine internals, fixture collection authenticity and business/accuracy impact. **OWNER DECISION:** release focus/media inputs, research system, engine coverage, evaluation budget and success threshold. ## Proposed research safeguards — ticket #6

**Decision: retain a combined minimum policy for later evaluation, with claim-level primary-source checks, conditional operator-aware corroboration and preserved retrieval evidence. Defer automatic ownership detection, blanket source-count thresholds and system integration until supported.** This resolves the planning comparison, not implementation or measured effectiveness. The evidence above establishes citation concentration, disclosed shared operation, incomplete collection provenance and outcome/attribution limits. It does **not** establish that every network claim is false, that all cited sources were fabricated, or that a particular engine used source counting as its ranking mechanism.

All mechanisms below are **proposals/inferences from that evidence**. **SUPPORTED** means the evidence gives a reason to consider a mechanism; it does not mean a trial proved its accuracy benefit. **PROVEN** applies here only to the existing fixture's numerical replay. Safeguard effectiveness and integration in an owner-selected research system are **UNKNOWN**. Costs are qualitative effort estimates, not measured prices; no paid tests were run.
