## Decision

**Accept the reproduced citation counts as facts about the released dataset. Retain the experiments as limited, operator-reported findings. Do not accept the video's universal retrieval mechanism, profitability, research-publisher identity, or recommendation-impact claims as established.**

The evidence supports investigating both legitimate brand visibility and research reliability. It does not establish a useful marketing return for our brands, nor prove that mass-generated pages changed recommendations. Reproducing a dataset's arithmetic does not authenticate its collection.

Classification: **REPRODUCED** means independently calculated from downloaded records; **SUPPORTED** means a primary artifact supports the narrow statement; **REPORTED** means published by the investigator without independent raw-data reproduction here; **UNKNOWN** means not established in this bounded investigation.

## Observable outcomes and attribution limits — ticket #4

**Decision: measure only observable stages; label unobserved stages unknown.** These are proposed operational definitions for later evaluation, not measured results for an owner-selected brand. Crawling, citation, mention, recommendation and referral are separate observations, not a guaranteed funnel. A recommendation can occur without a citation or a fresh fetch; a citation can support a competitor. No proprietary engine logs or hidden retrieval/ranking states are assumed.

These are counterexamples permitted by the definitions, not reported experiment outcomes. ### What counts can and cannot answer

For answer-based rates, declare the target entity/URL set, prompt set, engine/surface, observation dates and number of complete eligible answers. Report positive counts, observed negatives, unknown captures and exclusions separately. Repeated prompts, multiple citations in one answer, model tiers and shared-owner domains must not become independent people or corroboration. Request counts, answer counts and visit counts use different denominators; do not calculate stage-to-stage conversion without linked observations and compatible coverage. This is a measurement contract for later planning, not a selection of engines, prompts or sample size.

The reproduced 181/7,534 describes citation share in the released sample. It does not reveal brand-mention rate, recommendation lift, audience reach, attributable referrals, conversions, revenue, profit or research accuracy. Even authentic before/after visibility increases need a credible comparison to support causal claims. For the marketing track, useful value requires an owner-selected objective and evidence of appropriate recommendations and/or attributable qualified visits, with conversions and costs measured separately if business return is claimed. For the reliability track, value requires independently checked claim correctness and source support; popularity or citation volume cannot substitute for those checks.

## Proposed research safeguards — ticket #6

**Decision: retain a combined minimum policy for later evaluation, with claim-level primary-source checks, conditional operator-aware corroboration and preserved retrieval evidence. Defer automatic ownership detection, blanket source-count thresholds and system integration until supported.** This resolves the planning comparison, not implementation or measured effectiveness. The evidence above establishes citation concentration, disclosed shared operation, incomplete collection provenance and outcome/attribution limits. It does **not** establish that every network claim is false, that all cited sources were fabricated, or that a particular engine used source counting as its ranking mechanism.

All mechanisms below are **proposals/inferences from that evidence**. **SUPPORTED** means the evidence gives a reason to consider a mechanism; it does not mean a trial proved its accuracy benefit. **PROVEN** applies here only to the existing fixture's numerical replay. Safeguard effectiveness and integration in an owner-selected research system are **UNKNOWN**. Costs are qualitative effort estimates, not measured prices; no paid tests were run.

### Proposed minimum decision path

For a material conclusion, record the exact claim, its scope/date and supporting passage or calculation. Prefer the original artifact and assess whether its method supports that conclusion. Record operator relationships only to the extent evidenced; group declared shared operation for corroboration while preserving distinct datasets. Trace copied claims to their originating evidence even when publishers have different owners. Where a disputed or generalized conclusion needs corroboration, seek independent evidence collected or checked by appropriately independent operators; if unavailable, qualify or defer that conclusion. Do not select a numeric source minimum here.

Retain a URL, access time, relevant source excerpt or permitted snapshot, and the answer/claim-to-source mapping where available. Preserve input hashes and calculation commands for numerical work. Distinguish historical capture, arithmetic replay and a new live fetch. Record missing evidence as unknown with its consequence for the conclusion. These are proposed manual steps; the target's tools, permissions and retained data must be inspected before any automation claim.

### Cheapest falsifier: four decision cases

These are planning counterexamples, not new empirical trials. A policy that fails any case is unsuitable as written.

- **Uncertain ownership:** two domains share hosting and similar biographies but have no verified common-control evidence. Proposed result: ownership **UNKNOWN**, no automatic rejection or assertion of independence; inspect their evidence chains. The eight facts-page declarations above support grouping those declared operators, not extrapolating ownership to unrelated sites or earlier dates.
- **Copied claims across operators:** two independently owned sites repeat the same statistic from one original study. Proposed result: one originating evidence chain, not two independent measurements. Inspect the original method; repeated agreement does not validate the statistic. This is an illustrative case, not a finding that those two sites exist in the fixture.
- **Legitimate single-source fact:** the saved fixture's citation multiset yields 181/7,534 occurrences for the specified three domains. Proposed result: accept that narrow reproduced arithmetic without demanding another operator's dataset; collection authenticity and global prevalence remain **UNKNOWN**. Likewise, a provider's published bot definition is evidence of that provider's statement, not proof of every request's identity.
- **Source replay:** saved inputs and a command yield the same aggregate; a later page or live answer differs. Proposed result: the saved aggregate remains reproducible for that capture, while current content and original collection authenticity require separate evidence. Hash equality cannot authenticate a fabricated observation, and a new answer is not an exact replay of an old engine state.

## Assertions excluded from the resolution

- A crawler hit proves only a request, not an answered user question, citation, recommendation or conversion. The video's 320-question/one-human anecdote lacks an authenticated response-to-log linkage here.
- Specific universal rules that engines rank author bios/freshness, count sources instead of authenticating them, or evade Google filters because visitors are absent were not demonstrated by these studies. They are mechanistic hypotheses.
- The Zapier 50,000-page and two-thirds self-ranking assertions were not central numerical artifacts reproduced here; they cannot justify implementation without their own methods/denominators.
- Shared-source deduplication, primary-source preference and retrieval logging are proposed safeguards compared in ticket #6 above. None of these studies measures their effectiveness; operator agreement alone does not prove a claim true.
